Brush over it. It's gone.

Paint over whatever is ruining the shot and our generative model rebuilds what was behind it. Not a blur, not a patch copied from somewhere else in the frame: a new background that fits.

Under the hood

One AI engine, built in-house.

The inpainting engine behind Cleanup.photo is our own, served from our own infrastructure and delivered as a lightweight SDK. CreatePosts uses the same engine to clean photos before they're published.

  • Rebuilds the background instead of blurring or copying a patch
  • Runs on our servers, so any device gets the same speed
  • The whole editor embeds with a few lines of code
editor.js 
// Embed the cleanup editor anywhere
Inpaint.init({ apiKey: 'pk_live_…' });

Inpaint.open({
  container: '#editor',
  onResult: (blob) => save(blob),
});
