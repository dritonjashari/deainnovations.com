Privacy

This page explains what happens to your data when you visit deainnovations.com or work with us. Short version: very little, and only what's needed.

Last updated: 14 October 2026

This website

  • We don't use cookies, analytics or any kind of tracking.
  • Nothing is loaded from third parties: the fonts, images and code are all served from this domain.
  • There are no forms on this site, so we don't collect anything you type.

Server logs

Like almost every website, our hosting provider may record basic technical information about requests (such as IP address, browser type and the time of the request) to keep the site secure and running. These logs are not used to identify or profile visitors.

Emails

If you email us, we use your address and message only to reply to you. We don't sell or share them.

Client projects

Data you share with us for a project (such as access to your systems, content or social media accounts) is used only for that project and is kept no longer than the work requires.

Our products

Each product has its own terms for the data it processes. Cleanup.photo's policy is at cleanup.photo/privacy. Data you add to CreatePosts is used only to provide the service to you.

Questions and requests

To ask about your data, or to have something deleted, write to info@deainnovations.com.
