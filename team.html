About us

Software that does the busywork.

DEA Innovations is a software company. We help businesses build websites, apps and the software they need, automate the work that slows them down, and get found online through social media and SEO.

We design, build and run everything ourselves, including our own products. Cleanup.photo is free because it's the clearest way to show what we can build.

  • Useful AI, not AI for show

    We use AI where it saves real time: rebuilding a background, rewriting a post, answering the email that comes in every day.

  • Privacy by default

    Your data is used for your project and nothing else. Photos cleaned on Cleanup.photo are never kept in a gallery or used to train models.

  • Built to run

    Background jobs, retries and alerts mean the work gets done even when something on the internet doesn't cooperate.
